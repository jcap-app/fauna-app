Privacy Policy

Effective & last updated · July 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal data is collected, used, and handled when you use the Fauna mobile application (“Fauna” or the “App”).

1. Who we are (Data Controller)

Fauna (“Fauna,” “we,” “us,” “our”) is a mobile application that uses artificial intelligence to help people interpret animal behavior. The data controller responsible for your personal data is Jason Capostagno, an individual sole proprietor based in New York, USA (to be replaced with “Fauna LLC” once the LLC is formed).

  • Contact / data-protection requests: fauna.app.support@gmail.com
  • Postal address: available on request at the email above

We do not have an establishment in the European Union and have not appointed an EU/UK representative under Article 27 GDPR; given the nature and scale of processing we rely on the Article 27(2) derogation where applicable. If our processing changes, we will appoint a representative and update this policy.

2. Summary (the short version)

  • Fauna is local-first: your logs, photos, audio, video, notes, and daily check-ins live on your device.
  • To make sync, teams, animal-sharing, community, and account-recovery features work, Fauna automatically creates a lightweight anonymous account on our servers (hosted by Supabase). We therefore do operate a database, and the data it holds is linked to your anonymous account (see Section 3 for exactly what is stored).
  • To generate an interpretation, the content you submit is sent off your device to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) by way of a processing proxy (hosted on Cloudflare). This involves a transfer to the United States.
  • Anonymized behavioral observations are contributed to community statistics and research by default. This never includes your name, your animal’s name, or your media — and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or tracking.
  • Fauna does not provide veterinary or medical advice. See our Terms of Service.

3. What data we process

Fauna is local-first, but some data is stored on our servers (Supabase) so that sync, teams, sharing, community, account recovery, and crash-fixing can work. Data we store on our servers is linked to your anonymous account identifier.

a) Your account. When you first open Fauna, we create a lightweight anonymous account — no email or password required. It has a random account identifier and a shareable Fauna ID so teammates and shared-animal collaborators can find you. You may optionally secure your account with Sign in with Apple; if you do, Apple shares your name and email address (your real email, or Apple’s private “Hide My Email” relay address) with us so we can recover your account on a new device. Your profile also includes a display name you choose.

b) Content you create (“Observation Content”). Free-text behavior descriptions, photos, video recordings, audio recordings, daily check-ins, animal profile details (name, species, breed, age, weight, notes, routine), and accuracy ratings/feedback you give. This is stored locally on your device. Some of it is also stored on our servers when you use a feature that requires it, specifically:

  • Animals and observations you share (via a team or an Animal ID) — the animal profile, its photo, and the behavior observations shared to that team/collaboration are stored on our servers so the people you approve can see them.
  • Animal profile photos and team logos you upload are stored in our media storage.
  • Anonymized observations you contribute to community/research (on by default; see Section 4 and the opt-out below).
  • Achievements/badges you earn are synced so they survive a reinstall.

When you request an interpretation, what is sent off your device depends on the type:

  • Text, photos, and video are transmitted to our AI processor to generate the interpretation. (For video, we extract still frames on your device and send those frames.)
  • Audio recordings are processed on your device into rough metadata only (approximate recording length and a loudness estimate derived from file size) — the raw audio file is not transmitted to the AI processor; it stays on your device unless you delete it. Only that metadata and any text context you add are sent.

c) Teams and team clients (business/professional users). If you create or join a team, we store your team membership, role, and the team’s shared animals and observations. If you use the client-management feature, we store the client records you enter — which may include a client’s name, phone number, and email address. If you enter another person’s contact details, you are responsible for having a lawful basis to do so; you act as the controller of that client data and Fauna acts as your processor (a data-processing addendum is available on request).

d) Device and app data. App settings, subscription tier/entitlement state, and locally stored counters (e.g. monthly log counts). Subscription status is managed through Apple’s App Store / Google Play and your RevenueCat-validated entitlement; RevenueCat receives your purchase and entitlement history tied to your account identifier. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

e) Push notification token (if you enable notifications). We store your device’s push token (linked to your account) to deliver reminders/alerts you have opted into.

f) Diagnostic / crash data. To keep the app reliable, if the app hits an error we send a crash report to our servers (Supabase) containing a technical error message, a code stack trace, the platform, and the app version, linked to your anonymous account identifier. These reports are stripped of your content — they never include your notes, observations, animal names, or media — and are used solely to diagnose and fix bugs.

We do not intentionally collect special-category data about you. Because Observation Content is free text, please do not enter sensitive personal information about identifiable people.

5. Daily check-ins and the “Guardian” pattern read

Fauna offers a daily check-in (a short, structured pulse on your animal or colony) and a Living Dossier with an AI pattern read (“the Guardian”) that looks at your check-in history over time and surfaces steady baselines and changes worth watching.

  • Your check-in data is stored locally on your device. It is mirrored to our servers only for animals you share (team or Animal ID), so collaborators can see the shared record.
  • The Guardian pattern read sends a short text summary of your check-in trends (and, if you added them, your recent notes) to our AI processor to produce the read. It follows the same AI-processing terms as Section 6.
  • The check-in and Guardian features are non-diagnostic. They read your own records; they do not diagnose disease and are not veterinary advice. For any health concern, consult a licensed veterinarian (or, for a colony, a mentor/inspection). See our Terms of Service.

6. AI processing and third parties

To generate interpretations and pattern reads, the Observation Content you submit (text, photos, and video frames — and for audio, only on-device-derived metadata plus your text context, not the raw audio file, per Section 3) is transmitted from your device, authenticated as your account, to:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosts the processing proxy through which requests pass. See cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Anthropic, PBC — the AI provider that generates the interpretation. See anthropic.com/legal/privacy

We also use:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication (your anonymous account), and media storage.
  • RevenueCat — validates your subscription purchases/entitlements.
  • Expo / Apple / Google — app delivery, over-the-air updates, and push notification delivery.

This data leaves your device when you use these features. We pass your content to these providers solely to produce the output or run the feature you requested. We do not control, and cannot guarantee, how the AI providers log, cache, or retain data on their own systems; please review their respective policies. We instruct our providers not to use submitted content to train their models where such controls are available to us, but you should not rely on this for confidential material.

We do not use any analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

7. International data transfers

We are in the United States, and our providers (Supabase, Anthropic, Cloudflare, RevenueCat) may process data in the United States and other countries. If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, your data is therefore transferred outside your home region.

Where required, such transfers are made under appropriate safeguards, including the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and/or the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension, as offered by the relevant provider. You may request information about the safeguards in place by contacting us at fauna.app.support@gmail.com.

8. Where your data is stored and for how long

  • Observation Content on your device stays there until you delete it (delete a log, an animal, or use “Clear All” in Settings) or uninstall the app.
  • Data on our servers (your anonymous account/profile, shared animals and observations, team data and client records, media you uploaded, achievements, push token, and crash reports) is retained while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated server data at any time in Settings → Delete Account & Data; if you own a team, ownership passes to your next-highest-ranking member. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete server data — use Delete Account & Data (or email us).
  • Content sent to our AI processor for an interpretation is retained by that processor according to their retention policies (see Section 6), not ours.
  • Subscription/entitlement records are retained by Apple/Google/RevenueCat per their policies and our need to honor your purchases.
  • Crash reports are retained only as long as needed to diagnose stability issues.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal data.

EEA / UK / Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR): access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. You can exercise access and erasure directly in the app: your on-device content via the in-app delete tools, and your server-side data via Settings → Delete Account & Data. You can object to community/research contribution at any time via Settings → Privacy. For anything else we or our processors hold, contact us at fauna.app.support@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): the right to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of “sale”/“sharing.” We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined under the CPRA, and we do not process it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

To make a request, email fauna.app.support@gmail.com. We will verify your request and respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (generally within one month under GDPR; 45 days under the CCPA, extendable).

10. Children

Fauna is not directed to children. You must be at least 13 years old (16 in the EEA/UK) to use Fauna. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the applicable age. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact fauna.app.support@gmail.com and we will delete it.
